# Landmark card illustrations

Mode: built-in image_gen. Twelve 4×4 atlases. Row-major tile mapping by stable card ID: scripts/landmark-art.json. Stylized artwork, not archival photographs or measured architectural reconstructions; architectural detail remains subject to editorial review.
